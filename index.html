The challenge

Pakistan’s forest cover is just 4.8%, among the lowest in South Asia. Illegal logging, wildfires, and weak enforcement drive environmental degradation and climate disasters.

The initiative

Eco Muhafiz is a solar-powered, AI-enabled device that detects threats like chainsaws and fires, sending real-time alerts to authorities for rapid response.

The impact

Enhanced surveillance, safer patrolling, and quicker action against illegal activity. Each unit covers 5 km, promoting data-driven conservation and public trust.
